I have suffered anxiety for quite some time. It started small: did I close the door,
did I turn off the taps. Then it gradually became a nuisance. Then it blew up: I would
feel very anxious if I had no work, and I would feel very anxious if I had some work.
The feeling became permanent in the chest. No rest either way.

Today, for the first time in many months, I don't feel the tightness in the chest, the
anxiety associated with having work or no work. Such feeling only lasted 2 hours after a
good run before today.

What happened, I asked myself? Nothing actually, apart from the fact that during my run
yesterday, I slipped, fell, grazed myself badly, bloody. The fall itself was not bad, I
got up, and finished. Pace and distance normal.

So could the anxiety lift because I am in a bit of pain? Is this why people self harm:
the physical pain eases the mental pain?
