_Đề thi văn tốt nghiệp phổ thông 2026 gây xôn xao Steve Jobs. Không thấy ai bàn câu đầu
tiên: “Năm 1440, tại một xưởng cơ khí nhỏ nằm lọt thỏm giữa thành phố Mainz nước Đức,
Johannes Gutenberg phát minh ra máy in” là hư cấu, năm 1440, Gutenberg đang ở
Strasbourg._

People were applauding the exam paper for its up to date AI discussion. Somehow I read
the first line, and I knew something was off. Too precise, too smooth, too suspicious: a
small workshop, tucked away in Mainz, almost 600 years ago. The Wikipedia article, read
past its first line and down to its sources, says: in 1440 he was in
[Strasbourg](https://www.gutenberg-gesellschaft.de/en/johannes-gutenberg/gutenberg-in-strasbourg);
only in [1448](https://guides.loc.gov/gutenberg/the-printer) did he move back to Mainz.
Only one could be correct.

If I was a teacher, and some student wrote “In 1440…”, I would underline it immediately:
sources? Then if they cited sources, I would check the sources, and underline them:
rhetorical, not historical.

The extracts have not been fact checked, and it should not be down to me to do that. How
do they expect examinees to write about working with AI, while they have not been working
with AI themselves? If the setters used AI, they could fact check the text, just like me,
in a minute.

Perhaps it is the habit of the literature ones, who thought they could write their way
out of everything: write “Năm 1440, tại một xưởng cơ khí nhỏ…” and get away with it,
because it feels so right. Or “Hamlet có lẽ đã tan biến vào hư không ngay sau khi tấm màn
nhung của nhà hát Globe vừa khép lại”. The Globe was an open-air theatre; it had no velvet
curtain to close. The velvet curtain is a writer's trick, a detail to make you see the
ending. Those tricks are about to get trampled over: here come AIs that can write
smoother, faster, even more accurate. A sentence that sounds that smooth is now a sign for
checking, not a sign of craft.

The [official answer guide](https://dcdn.dantri.com.vn/2026/06/19/1-dap-an-ngu-van-1781865369049.pdf)
says young people should “biết kiểm chứng, phản biện thông tin do AI cung cấp”. Nobody
applied it to the extract; the same guide makes students quote the velvet curtain as
evidence. They don't practise what they preach. And the irony is in the text itself:
“dân chúng không có quyền tiếp cận văn bản gốc”, when the sources of the text are
themselves dubious.
