The trip to Vietnam this summer was a success, at least from the point of view
of the grandmas. They got to see their grandson, who, weirdly enough, showed
them more affection than the ones that are in Vietnam. The grandson would
happily eat whatever they cooked (they do not cook often, their family
complaining about the food). He even said “Bà nấu ngon mà”; perhaps nobody had
said that to her for a very long time.

I myself did not understand the attachment to food, always “Ăn thêm đi con”,
and the like. But then I looked at the squirrels in the garden. When they ate,
they looked happy, and I felt warm in the heart; perhaps that's how we looked to
the grandmas, and how they felt as well.
